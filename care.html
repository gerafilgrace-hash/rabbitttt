Rabbit Habitat

Where Do Rabbits Live?

Rabbits live in grasslands, forests, meadows, and areas with bushes. Many wild rabbits dig underground burrows for shelter and protection.

What Do Rabbits Eat?

Rabbits eat grass, leafy plants, herbs, and other vegetation.

What Do Rabbits Need?

Rabbits need food, clean water, shelter, space to move, and protection from predators.

Fun Fact

Some rabbits live in underground tunnel systems called warrens.
